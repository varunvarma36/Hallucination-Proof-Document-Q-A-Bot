PART II
Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights:
Total net sales decreased 2.8% or $11.0 billion during fiscal year 2023 compared to fiscal year 2022, driven primarily by lower net sales of Mac, Wearables, Home and Accessories and iPad, partially offset by an increase in Services net sales. Products net sales decreased 5.7% or $18.1 billion, while Services net sales increased 9.0% or $7.1 billion.

Research and Development (R&D) Expense Breakdown:
The Company continues to invest in research and development to support new technologies and product enhancements. Total research and development expense was $29,915 million for fiscal year 2023, representing approximately 7.8% of total net sales. R&D spending increased by $3,664 million or 14% year-over-year, driven primarily by headcount-related expenses and project-related engineering costs.
